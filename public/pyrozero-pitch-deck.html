01 / THE PROBLEM
PyroZero

The lunar fire trap

Earth-test behavior can change in a reduced-gravity, oxygen-enriched environment.

34% O₂

At 8.2 psia / 56.5 kPa.
About 62% higher oxygen fraction than 21% air.

0.165g

Enough buoyant flow to replenish oxygen, with less convective heat loss.

70%

Of subsystems assessed in one NASA ECLSS study require material replacement.

The research bottleneck

Terabytes of PSI research must be matched to fuel, atmosphere, flow, and scale. Partial-gravity coverage remains sparse; simulated-lunar LUCI evidence already exists.

NASA NTRS 20260003925; 20240015307; NASA PSI update (July 2025); LUCI flight summary. Some cool-flame regimes in the supplied records are near 800 K; flame appearance alone is not a safety test.

05 / THE IMPACT
PyroZero

Space lessons, Earth applications

Apollo 1 · Apollo 13 · Mir

Trace discrete report anchors, fuel context, containment, ignition, and detection lessons.

Artemis II appears separately as an annotated smoke-alarm case. It is not treated as a confirmed spacecraft fire.

Fine water mist

Supplied D₁₀ = 28 μm and D₃₂ = 46 μm references. Explore surface area and droplet diameter.

Potential: compact suppression systems.
650 nm particle optics

Polarization and multiple scattering angles help explain dust, smoke, and mist discrimination.

Potential: demanding industrial environments.
4,500 psi composite SCBA

Compare stored air with legacy cylinders; distinguish PBI turnout fabrics from Beta cloth.

Potential: firefighter protection research.

Demonstrations and potential applications—not CFD, certified detectors, fire-extinguisher approval, or SCBA service-duration guarantees. Li-ion/data-center and mine use require dedicated testing.
