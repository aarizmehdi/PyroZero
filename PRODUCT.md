# PyroZero

An engineering research workspace by Zentrix Labs for comparing NASA PSI combustion investigations, screening bounded mission atmospheres, tracing reported incidents, and querying citation-grounded evidence.

Users are combustion researchers, mission safety engineers, and technical reviewers. Four modules share a mission envelope. Artemis lunar is the initial environment. No material is selected or calculated until the user explicitly chooses one. Presets are bounded proxies, including EVA at 85% oxygen. Results are deterministic research screens; physical qualification remains separate.

The calibrated SIBAL coefficient is 0.3147. Physics equations, API contract, and canonical catalogue are preserved during interface work. Scientific terminology and evidence remain visible with definitions and progressive disclosure.
